Privacy Policy

Build Tower
Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy describes how the Android game Build Tower (package name com.com.beauchicllc.BuildTower) handles information. The game is published by LinkVN (“we”).

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on the device only, using local storage:

  • Your current level
  • Points added by in-app purchases

This information stays on the device. The game does not upload it to a server we operate. Uninstalling the game deletes this local data.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or Google account password. Google Play tells the game whether a purchase succeeded so the corresponding points can be added on the device. Purchase records are kept by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

The game does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID. It does not show third-party ads, and it does not send analytics events to an analytics service.

A button in the game may open our Facebook page in the browser. That visit is handled by Facebook after you leave the game.

Children

Build Tower is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Delete local progress and purchased points stored by the game by uninstalling it, or by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings. Purchases already made remain in your Google Play account and can be restored by Google Play if you install the game again.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
